Candlepage: privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Candlepage does not sell or share personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. By default nothing leaves your browser. The only data that can be sent to the developer is the optional failure report described below, and only if you turn it on.

  • Your PDFs are opened and rendered inside your browser. They are never uploaded.
  • Settings (dark mode, brightness, language) are saved with Chrome storage and may sync through your own Chrome account if you have sync on.
  • Saved books, history and formulas are kept locally in your browser and can be deleted from the extension window.
  • Math tools run on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Google Drive button only runs on drive.google.com and adds a button that opens the file in the viewer. It does not read or send your Drive data to us.
  • Bug report is optional: it prepares an email and a text file on your computer. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.
  • Anonymous failure reports (optional, off by default). The first time you open the extension window it asks whether you want to send them; you can change your answer at any time in the same window, and "What would be sent" shows the latest report. When this is on, and a PDF cannot be opened or some of its pages cannot be drawn, the extension sends one small report to a server run by the developer (Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1). You can also send a single report by hand from the error message, after seeing its text.
    • What a report contains: the extension and Chrome version, operating system, language and window size; how the PDF was embedded in the page (top level, frame, size, sandboxed or not); the website's origin (for example https://example.com) and a generic shape of the file's path in which names and numbers are replaced (for example /files/:id/:name.pdf); the names, not the values, of the address's query parameters; the type and status of the answers the website gave when the file was requested in several ways; a shortened error message with addresses removed; the number of pages and how many failed; and a random anonymous install ID.
    • What it never contains: the PDF or any of its content, its file name, the full address or query values, page text or titles, cookies, or your name, email or account.
    • Purpose: only to find and fix PDFs that do not open. Reports are not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and are not sold or shared.
    • Limits: at most one report per kind of failure and 20 a day. Your IP address is not stored in the database; Cloudflare may keep its own technical logs of requests, including IP addresses, for a limited time.
    • Control and deletion: you can turn reports off at any time, and reset your anonymous ID in the extension window. To have stored reports deleted, email the address below with your anonymous ID (shown in "What would be sent").
  • Donation link opens ko-fi.com in a new tab only if you click it.
