Candlepage: privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Candlepage does not sell or share personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. By default nothing leaves your browser. The only data that can be sent to the developer is the optional failure report described below, and only if you turn it on.

  • Your PDFs are opened and rendered inside your browser. They are never uploaded.
  • Settings (dark mode, brightness, language) are saved with Chrome storage and may sync through your own Chrome account if you have sync on.
  • Saved books, history and formulas are kept locally in your browser and can be deleted from the extension window.
  • PDFs on the page you are viewing. On web pages, a small script in the extension counts the links to PDFs and embedded PDFs so the toolbar button can show a number. This happens on your device: only the number is passed to the extension's own background, and no addresses, text or page content are stored or sent anywhere. When you open the extension window it lists the PDFs found on the current tab; for a frame whose address only might be a PDF, the extension asks that website for the first few bytes of the address to check, as your browser would, and sends nothing to the developer.
  • Welcome PDF shown after installing is part of the extension and opens locally.
  • Dark mode and colours are worked out on your device while each page is drawn. The colours and pictures of your PDF are never sent anywhere by this.
  • Math tools run on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Google Drive button only runs on drive.google.com and adds a button that opens the file in the viewer. It does not read or send your Drive data to us.
  • Bug report is optional: it prepares an email and a text file on your computer. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.
  • Anonymous failure reports (optional, off by default). The viewer asks once, right after the first-run tour, whether you want to send them; you can change your answer at any time in the extension window, and "What would be sent" shows the latest report. When this is on, and a PDF cannot be opened or some of its pages cannot be drawn, the extension sends one small report to a server run by the developer (Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1). You can also send a single report by hand from the error message, after seeing its text.
    • What a report contains: the extension and Chrome version, operating system, language and window size; how the PDF was embedded in the page (top level, frame, size, sandboxed or not); the website's origin (for example https://example.com) and a generic shape of the file's path in which names and numbers are replaced (for example /files/:id/:name.pdf); the names, not the values, of the address's query parameters; the type and status of the answers the website gave when the file was requested in several ways; a shortened error message with addresses removed; the number of pages and how many failed; and an anonymous install ID. The ID is issued by the report server the first time a report is sent (your browser first does a small calculation, so that fake IDs cannot be made in bulk) and is kept with a signed proof; it is not linked to you.
    • What it never contains (except the pictures in a colour report that you looked at and sent yourself): the PDF or any of its content, its file name, the full address or query values, page text or titles, cookies, or your name, email or account.
    • Colour reports: in Features and settings, "Colours look wrong?" lets you send one report by hand about the current page. It contains only the page number, page count, picture-colour mode and the sizes and positions (as fractions of the page) of the regions kept in their original colours. When the colour notice's "Help fix this" button is pressed, a small picture of the flagged area (or of the page) is already added, in two versions (as printed and as dark mode shows it); you see it before anything is sent and can remove it or choose a smaller area instead, and nothing is sent until you press Send. You can also drag a box around the area that looks wrong to add two small pictures of just that area, as printed in the PDF and as dark mode shows it; it is shown to you first and you can remove it. The report then also holds a short written finding about that area (for example, that it is shown as a negative) with a few numbers, and a short fingerprint of the file that cannot be turned back into the file and only lets the developer tell whether two reports are about the same document. You see everything before it is sent, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.
    • Automatic colour notes (only if you turned anonymous reports on): when the extension notices that a page may have lost its picture colours or may show wrong colours, it sends one short note with the same numbers as a colour report (page number, page count, picture-colour mode and the sizes and positions of the kept regions). It never contains a picture or any content from the PDF; pictures are sent only when you choose an area and press Send. The choices "Keep picture colours" and "No picture colours in this PDF" are saved only in your browser, per document, and are not sent.
    • Purpose: only to find and fix PDFs that do not open or that are shown with wrong colours. Reports are not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and are not sold or shared.
    • Limits: at most one report per kind of failure and 20 a day. Your IP address is not stored in the database; Cloudflare may keep its own technical logs of requests, including IP addresses, for a limited time.
    • Bug reports: "Report a bug" in the extension window can send the short note you type (up to 400 characters), the extension version, your browser and system type, and the last loading problem the extension recorded (no PDF content, no file name). Nothing is sent until you press Send. You can also choose email instead, which sends nothing from the extension.
    • If a report cannot be delivered (the server is busy or you are offline), it stays in the extension's local storage and is retried now and then, for at most 3 days and 6 tries, then deleted. At most 5 wait at a time. Reports you did not agree to send automatically are never retried after you turn reports off.
    • Retention: pictures in colour reports are deleted automatically after 30 days, and all reports after 12 months.
    • Control and deletion: you can turn reports off at any time, and reset your anonymous ID in the extension window. To have stored reports deleted, email the address below with your anonymous ID (shown in "What would be sent").
  • Donation link opens ko-fi.com in a new tab only if you click it.
